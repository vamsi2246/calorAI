## 🎨 Design Guidelines for Screenshots
1. **Device Mockups**: Wrap screenshots in device frame templates (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro) to improve visual presentation in the repository description.
2. **Theme Consistency**: Ensure all screenshots are captured in dark mode to preserve the app's dark theme aesthetic.
3. **High-Resolution**: Capture images at 3x resolution to keep typography crisp.
